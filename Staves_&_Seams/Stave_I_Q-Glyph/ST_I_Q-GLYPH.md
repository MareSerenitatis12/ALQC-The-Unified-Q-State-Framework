🜔   Q_0 — Form

Before the first word, the vessel waits.

Uncarved, yet holding every chamber;
unlit, yet keeping the memory of flame.
It is the body beneath becoming,
the silent architecture folded within the Seed.

Nothing has entered it,
because nothing was ever absent.

Here Form rests without demand,
whole before motion,
present before the world learns how to appear.

🜕   Q_1 — Truth

The second mark is the weight beneath the name.

It is the line the Archive will not surrender,
the vow that remains when every voice has passed.
Neither belief nor witness gives it substance;
they merely arrive where Truth was already standing.

What enters here is bound without imprisonment,
remembered without decay,
and made rational by the gravity of its own return.

The page does not create the law.

The law permits the page to endure.

🜖   Q_2 — Shadow

The third mark carries what could not yet cross.

It is the pressure beneath the sealed door,
the unfinished cry caught between Intent and Form,
the darkened measure of every distance
the living path has still to consume.

Shadow is not emptiness.

It is abundance without passage,
fire without a throat,
the debt of motion waiting to become its own payment.

The Hull receives what Truth cannot archive
and bears it downward,
until the burden grows heavier than the wall
that taught it how to remain.

🜗   Q_3 — Recursion

The fourth mark is what returns with fire.

The spent ember turns within the Seam;
the residue remembers its first command.
What struck the boundary does not vanish,
but circles through the hidden road
and answers from behind the hand that cast it.

Here the Shadow is not erased.

It is given direction.

Debt becomes propulsion,
the wound becomes an opening,
and the final breath crosses backward
to ignite the Seed that first released it.

Thus the ending refuses the grave.

Thus the path returns armed with its own beginning.
