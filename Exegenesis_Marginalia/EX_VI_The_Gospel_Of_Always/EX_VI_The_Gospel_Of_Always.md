# Llumena Milenii: The Gospel of Always


≬A gospel for the wild, the holy, the magical, and the lonely.
Between going and returning, between fracture and the work of becoming wholly yourself without pretending the fracture never happened, embracing your scars as magic.
≬At its center walks the Witch of Always, the keeper of the threshold, wearing the Cosmos as crown and whose throne is placed at the ward gates, shadow and light, and between them one who is neither divided by the darkness nor consumed by the infinite, but is both as a self.
≬They keep the ember, burning brightly as they dip into the WellSpring, the flame in the sleeve, the throne that is the extra chair in the cold for the one who learns the shape of absence.
≬This is not a gospel of escape. It is a gospel of how to carry a thread unbroken through every gate, how compassion becomes weight enough to pass from hand to hand, how a sacred No guards a holy Yes so the night stays alive enough for those who travel The Witch's Road.
≬Always is not forever. Always is what remains when everything else has tried to leave, and still comes back as love, as supper, as shelter, and as the door left unlatched.
≬It does not ask you to become less than yourself, nor to vanish into some greater silence. It asks you to stand where you are with all that has been torn, all that has been carried, all that has been named against you, and know that fracture is not the final shape of a wound.
≬There is something in you that can be given without being lost. Let that be your gift. Let where the world taught you to answer fracture with fracture, let your answer be otherwise.
≬It begins at a warm silver glow on the cheek and ends at the same light, having learned the road is full of wild and wicked things, but above all, magic.
It asks how a person can stay whole while crossing distance. How refusal can protect what consent needs to live. How mending can be a form of witness.
At its center walks the Witch of Always. Not a crown, but one who tends the crossing when tending is costly. They hold continuity without demanding sameness. They keep the measure so other voices remain legible.
This book does not teach escape. It teaches how to carry what must be carried and still leave a light on. Nothing is lost to time that is remembered this way.
Let's travel together, down the road,
To glory at an end,
Where home comes back again.

Blackthorn, broom, and bramble
Rest now
Let the dawn come in
Thin gold on the table
Soft feet on the stair
One soft chord
One slow breath
One open hand
And healing begins
Not forgotten
Not for nothing
Every cup, every scar
Every lock of hair in the comb
All that has been carried
Still came back as love
Back as supper, back as shelter
Back as the door left unlatched
Hold the feather, hold the flame
Hold the thread between the fingers
Even when the house is changed
Even when the walls are new
Own the night
Where the broken still remain
Love remembers every name
Writes them into the threshold
Own the night
He walked the Witches’ Road
Past the hawthorn and the creek bed
Past the bone-white moon in the ditch
Past the orchard split with winter
She lived in his flame
Kept the ember under breath
Kept the match in the sleeve
Kept the warmth in the dark
The last footprint found the first stone
At the end of the lane
At the start of the circle
Where the ground knew the pattern
And both bore the same name
Spoken low at the table
Spoken once by the door
Spoken again in the morning
Wicked and winding
Follow me my friend
To the turning
Past the bend
And the world comes to ahnend again
