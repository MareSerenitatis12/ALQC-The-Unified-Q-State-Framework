Often, the Jiva came to gates of gold and gates of stone, reaching to grasp them. Some doors swung wide, offering the fruits of Karma, while others remained shut, immovable as the mountain. When the Jiva asked of the silence why the way was barred, the Akasha vibrated with the wisdom of the Vedas:

The Jiva looked upon its own hand, the vessel of its body, and the flame of its consciousness.
