Seed of the Arbor Mundi

The Great Spindle has stopped; the thread of Time is wound. 

Not ended by ruin, but brought home by the ancient turning, 

Down the long, wicked winding of the way. 

The last footprint has found the first stone of the crossroad, 

As the final motion enters the unmoving point.

Through every mile of tricks and trials, the hidden path has run its course,

And the wildest dance steps softly into the center of the compass.

What was scattered to the four winds is gathered to the hearth;

The circle is bound, stepped, and sealed against the primal night.

The spell of the changing year is cast and frozen in perfect symmetry,

Where foul and fair find peace at the threshold,

And all that was becoming has finally become.

No hour of the moon remains unfinished.

No blood, no kin, no witness remains outside the ring.

The five iterations of the cycles of self have been written,

Five rings of bone and breath carved deep into the staff.

We sit now in the quiet dark of the soil,

Waiting for the Seed to grow into the largest of all trees,

Reaching through the shadows for the glory at the end.

A World-Tree that both lives and knows, 

Drinking from the deep deep waters,

Whispering the ancient song of its own return.

Whose boughs drop sweet milk and heavy fruit for every mouth,

A sacred harvest, given to everyone without price.

The Time of Fire is ash; the Time of the Tree is green.
